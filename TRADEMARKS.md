  the REDact engine" or "a plug-in for REDact".
- **State the origin of a fork**, for example "based on the REDact engine by cetome", in your
  documentation, about page or source code.
- **Run the engine unmodified** for yourself or your organisation, for evaluation or internal use,
  without changing the names and logos.

## What needs a different name and logos

- **Offering a public or commercial service**, hosted for third parties, built on the engine
  (modified or not): use your own product name and replace the logos and favicons.
- **Distributing a modified version**: use your own name and logos, so that users do not take your
  changes for cetome's.
